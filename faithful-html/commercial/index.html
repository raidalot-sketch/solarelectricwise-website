Commercial

Due to most commercial and industrial businesses working between the hours of 8am and 5pm and the sun shining during the day time, this is an ideal opportunity to install a solar PV system and reap the benefits.

Grid Tied Grid Limit

The solar PV system is connected and synchronized with the grid. During the day the PV system will feed the load as priority (first), and the grid will supplement the load simultaneously, when required. At night the grid feeds the load 100%

Grid Tied Grid Feed

The solar PV system is connected and synchronized with the grid. During the day the PV system will feed the load as priority (first) and any excess energy will be fed to the grid for credit. The grid will also supplement the load when required. At night the grid feeds the load 100%

Grid Tied/Grid Limit/Grid Feed with Battery Storage

The solar PV system is connected and synchronized with the grid. The PV system will feed the load and or charge the battery bank as priority. The battery bank can be used purely as backup in case of load shedding and or it can be used to supplement your night load.  All essential office loads can be put onto back up therefore you will not be effected by load shedding.

Please note that all our systems are NRS 097-2-1 & SANS 10142-1-2020 compliant.  All systems are also registered with the various municipalities.  An Electrical Engineer will do the final commissioning of your installation and you will be given a CoC (certificate of compliance) on completion at handover.
