About Us

SolarElectricWise

SolarElectricWise is a solar PV installation company, and we have been in the solar PV industry since 2015.  We are qualified, licensed, and registered with nearly 30 years experience in all aspects of the electrical field from domestic houses to commercial buildings and industrial sites.  We also have 10 years experience in the energy management industry.  

Our priority is to audit and present you, the client, with realistic savings and a quote using Tier 1 panels and well known other brands from our industry.

Our offer includes audits of your electrical consumption, and once the quote is accepted, we will install your system.  We also offer on going maintenance and servicing of your solar PV system at a small cost annually.

At SolarElectricWise our aim is to offer you, our client, quality service using the best equipment to achieve effective results and set a high standard for others to follow.

Do You Want To Make The Step To Sustainable Electricity ?

In South Africa, we receive abundant sunlight every day, and the vast majority of us are not making the best use of it. Why waste that energy? We at SolarElectricWise can help you make full use of this sunlight with our solar PV installations. Sunlight is radiant energy and it makes sense that we use this renewable energy source to its fullest. To have a solar electricity system fitted in your home that makes full use of solar energy will bring down your electricity bill radically, and at the same time, increase the value of your property. Every year, the knowledge around solar PV technology and equipment improves, bringing us equipment that is increasingly reliable and easier to control. Battery technology is also moving forward at a rapid pace and this technology will also continue to advance. So, the time in which you pay back your investment is getting increasingly shorter as equipment is becoming more efficient and the price of electricity rises. And the best part? Eskom load shedding will become a thing of the past, as will Eskom’s price increases, as you will no longer be fully dependent upon them to power your home.
