Our Story

Professional Solar PV Installers in Cape Town

SolarElectric is a trusted solar PV installation company in Cape Town, specialising in residential, commercial, and industrial solar energy systems throughout the Western Cape. Since 2015, we have provided reliable solar PV installations, battery backup systems, inverter installations, hybrid solar systems, and off-grid solar solutions — energy-saving solutions to reduce electricity bills and load shedding disruption.

Our qualified electricians and experienced installers are fully licensed and registered with the Department of Labour (DoL), with over 40 years of electrical industry experience, more than 10 years in energy management solutions, and 10 years in solar PV solutions. We provide professional, customised solar system designs and realistic energy-saving solutions using Tier 1 solar panels such as Aiko, Canadian and JA Solar, together with trusted inverter brands including Sunsynk, Deye, Sigenergy, Sungrow and Solis. We also supply advanced lithium battery solutions including Sungrow and SigenStor HV Li-Ion Stackable, and Eenovance LV Li-Ion battery systems.

SolarElectric offers SSEG and industry-compliant installations, with a Certificate of Compliance (COC) provided on completed installations as well as PR Electrical Engineer sign-off. Our professional installation team delivers quality workmanship backed by a workmanship warranty, together with ongoing after-sales support and maintenance to ensure your solar PV system operates efficiently and reliably for years to come.

Why Go Solar

Make The Step To Sustainable Electricity

South Africa receives abundant sunlight every day, and most of us aren't making the best use of it.

A solar electricity system fitted at your home makes full use of solar energy and will bring down your electricity bill radically, while increasing the value of your property. Solar PV technology and equipment keeps improving every year, becoming more reliable and easier to control, and battery technology continues to advance rapidly too.

The result: the payback period on your investment keeps shrinking as equipment gets more efficient and electricity prices rise. And best of all — Eskom load shedding and price increases become a thing of the past, as you're no longer fully dependent on the grid to power your home or business.
