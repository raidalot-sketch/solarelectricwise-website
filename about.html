Our Story

A Licensed Solar PV Installer Since 2015

SolarElectricWise is a solar PV installation company, and we have been in the solar PV industry since 2015. We are qualified, licensed and registered, with nearly 30 years of experience across all aspects of the electrical field — from domestic houses to commercial buildings and industrial sites. We also bring 10 years of experience in the energy management industry.

Our priority is to audit and present you, the client, with realistic savings and a quote using Tier 1 panels and other well-known brands from our industry. Our offer includes an audit of your electrical consumption, and once the quote is accepted, we install your system. We also offer ongoing maintenance and servicing of your solar PV system at a small annual cost.

At Solar Electric Wise, our aim is to offer you, our client, quality service using the best equipment to achieve effective results — and to set a high standard for others to follow. We're an EPC (Engineering, Procurement and Construction) company.

Why Go Solar

Make The Step To Sustainable Electricity

South Africa receives abundant sunlight every day, and most of us aren't making the best use of it.

A solar electricity system fitted at your home makes full use of solar energy and will bring down your electricity bill radically, while increasing the value of your property. Solar PV technology and equipment keeps improving every year, becoming more reliable and easier to control, and battery technology continues to advance rapidly too.

The result: the payback period on your investment keeps shrinking as equipment gets more efficient and electricity prices rise. And best of all — Eskom load shedding and price increases become a thing of the past, as you're no longer fully dependent on the grid to power your home or business.
