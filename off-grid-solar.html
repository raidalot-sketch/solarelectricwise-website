Off-Grid Systems

Solar Independence, Engineered To Last

Off-grid systems have no connection to Eskom at all — solar panels charge a battery bank that runs your property around the clock, with sizing based on a detailed audit of your daily electricity usage so you never run short.

We use Tier 1 panels paired with reliable battery and inverter technology, and because off-grid systems carry no safety net from the grid, correct sizing and quality components matter even more. Ongoing maintenance keeps your battery bank healthy for years to come.

  • Full energy audit to size panels, batteries and inverter correctly
  • Tier 1 panels with proven battery storage technology
  • Ideal for remote, rural or smallholding properties without reliable grid access
  • Complete independence from Eskom load shedding and price increases
  • Annual maintenance and servicing plans
