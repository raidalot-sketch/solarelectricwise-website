Home Solar Systems

Solar Designed Around How Your Household Uses Electricity

Every residential installation starts with an audit of your electrical consumption. From there we design a grid-tied or grid-feed system using Tier 1 panels and trusted inverter and battery brands, sized to genuinely reduce your monthly bill rather than oversell you equipment you don't need.

Once you accept your quote, our qualified team — backed by nearly 30 years of electrical experience — handles the full installation, and we offer ongoing maintenance and servicing afterwards at a small annual cost.

  • Free electrical consumption audit and system sizing
  • Tier 1 solar panels and reputable inverter/battery brands
  • Grid-tied and grid-feed (export back to the grid) configurations
  • Backup power options to ride through load shedding
  • Annual maintenance and servicing plans
