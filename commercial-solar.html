Business Solar Systems

Solar That Protects Your Bottom Line

As an EPC (Engineering, Procurement and Construction) company, we manage commercial solar projects end-to-end: auditing your site's electrical consumption, engineering a system sized to your actual demand, procuring Tier 1 panels and industrial-grade inverters and batteries, then installing and commissioning the system.

For businesses, downtime from load shedding and grid instability has a direct cost. A correctly sized grid-tied or grid-feed system reduces your Eskom draw, and where needed, battery backup keeps critical operations running through outages.

  • Site electrical audit and load analysis
  • System design for offices, retail, warehouses and industrial sites
  • Tier 1 panels and commercial-grade inverters and batteries
  • Grid-tied and grid-feed configurations
  • Ongoing maintenance and servicing contracts
